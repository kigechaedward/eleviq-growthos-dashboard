## 34. AI agent decision framework

1. Is this corporate or product-specific?
2. Which product is involved?
3. Is the project active?
4. Is the information current?

## 40. Canonical rule

Eleviq Technologies is the parent business.

MyHobbyPlan, Anitrace, LostNFoundHub, and future proprietary products are independent product businesses under the Eleviq umbrella unless explicitly separated.

- Corporate decisions may affect products.
- Product decisions do not automatically affect the corporation or other products.
- Shared systems may serve multiple products.
- Client projects remain separate from proprietary Eleviq products unless explicitly incorporated.
- The latest explicit decision from Edward is authoritative.
- When information is uncertain, do not invent it.
- When a strategic dependency exists, flag it.
- When research is required, research before making a factual claim.

The ultimate objective is to help Eleviq Technologies build a sustainable, scalable, technology-driven business with strong products, recurring revenue, effective marketing, and intelligent operations.
